Progress — six crews

Each of the six crews (2 sections × special crossings / trenchless / standard) keeps its own trench and pipe front. Progress in either direction stays on the same stretch; moving a crew elsewhere is a relocation — it opens a new stretch rather than crediting the ground in between.

Work already behind or ahead of each section's overall completed front, from any crew, is caught up on the catching-up tabs.
